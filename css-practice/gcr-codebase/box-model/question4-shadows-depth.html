Box-Shadow & Text-Shadow Breakdown

text-shadow: x y blur color;

Adds depth behind text glyphs. Unlike box shadows, text shadows take no spread radius and shape directly to the font character outlines.

box-shadow: x y blur spread color;

Defines offset-x, offset-y, blur-radius, and optional spread-radius. Stacking multiple comma-separated shadows produces natural, photo-realistic elevation.

Elevation on :hover

Pairing transform: translateY(-8px) with increased blur and spread simulates physical distance from the surface layer.
