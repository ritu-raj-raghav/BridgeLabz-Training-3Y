The Rhythm of Leading, Tracking & Hierarchy

Properly calibrating line-height (leading) and letter-spacing (tracking) prevents eye fatigue. When lines of text are spaced proportionally to their typeface's x-height, reader saccades flow naturally across the page. In this article, you will observe how custom highlighters guide user attention, while strong typographic anchors solidify conceptual focal points, and italicized emphasis introduces nuanced intonation into digital prose.
