Why Reading Matters

Reading opens a door to new ideas, places, and points of view. A few pages each day can build vocabulary, improve focus, and help us understand experiences beyond our own.

Choose books that interest you and make reading a regular habit. Even a short, consistent session can be useful over time.

“The more that you read, the more things you will know.”

When reading online, look for reliable sources and check important facts before sharing them.

Keep a book nearby and read for ten minutes today.
