Principles for Balanced Micro-Interactions

1. Subtlety First

Keep scale() values between 1.03 and 1.10 to prevent accidental layout obscuring or jarring optical vibrations.

2. Coordinated Shadows

When an element translates upwards, expand the blur and spread of its shadow to mimic real-world lighting.

3. Timing Curves

Use cubic bezier or ease-in-out timing curves around 0.3s for responsiveness without sluggishness.
